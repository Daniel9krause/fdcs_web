How it works

One photo, four answers

Capture or upload

Use your phone or laptop camera in the browser, or upload photos you took in the field — one at a time or many at once.

What's wrong?

A MobileNetV2 CNN checks the whole image and overlapping sections to detect pests and diseases and mark where they are.

Which organism?

Get the organism's name and classification — insect, mite, fungus, water mould, bacterium or virus — with its kingdom and family.

Safe to eat?

A clear verdict — safe, eat with care, or not safe — with what to cut away or throw out, plus treatment advice and supplies.
